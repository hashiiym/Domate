🗓 Connect Google Calendar
Follow these steps to get your Google OAuth Client ID. This is a one-time setup.
1

Go to Google Cloud Console, create a project (or pick an existing one).

2

Navigate to APIs & Services → Library, search for "Google Calendar API" and click Enable.

3

Go to APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen. Choose External, fill your app name & email, save. Under Scopes add:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events

4

Go to Credentials → Create Credentials → OAuth Client ID. Choose Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins add your site URL (or http://localhost for local testing).

5

Copy the Client ID and paste it below:

💡 No backend needed. Your Client ID is stored locally in your browser. Tasks sync directly to your personal Google Calendar.
